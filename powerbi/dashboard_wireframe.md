# Dashboard Wireframe

1. Insurance Executive — premium, claims, loss ratio, retention, fraud signals and risk exposure.
2. Portfolio Profitability — premium vs approved claims by product, geography and risk band.
3. Claims Intelligence — volume, severity, status, approval and settlement trends.
4. Customer & Retention — active/lapsed customers, policy depth and retention risk.
5. Risk Concentration — high/critical risk concentration by product, broker and geography.
6. Claims Investigation — flagged claims, claim-to-sum-insured ratio, payment anomalies and event activity.
7. Data Trust — duplicate, orphan, financial, temporal and reconciliation defects.
