# Portfolio Story

**Problem:** leadership lacks one trusted view across policy, claims, customer and risk domains.

**Investigation:** profiling exposed duplicate, orphan, temporal and financial defects that could distort loss and risk reporting.

**Transformation:** dbt standardised source data and created reusable analytical marts.

**Analysis:** SQL connected portfolio economics with claims, payments, customer retention and risk signals.

**Decision support:** Power BI surfaces executive performance, loss ratio, retention, risk concentration and investigation priorities.

**Outcome:** the project demonstrates movement from messy operational data to defensible, auditable decision support.
