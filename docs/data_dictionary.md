# Data Dictionary

- customers: customer master and retention attributes.
- policies: coverage, premium, sum insured, broker and risk attributes.
- policy_transactions: policy financial movements.
- claims: reported losses, approved values, severity and fraud signals.
- claim_events: claim lifecycle and investigation events.
- payments: claim-related financial movements.
- vehicles_assets: insured asset characteristics.
- brokers: distribution and broker attributes.
- risk_assessments: policy risk score, band and assessment source.
